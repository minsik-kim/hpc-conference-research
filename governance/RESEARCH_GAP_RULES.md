# RESEARCH_GAP_RULES

A research gap is not created by the mere absence of a topic in this
repository. Every candidate gap must pass through this pipeline before it
is treated as a real, surviving research question:

```
observation
      ↓
CANDIDATE
      ↓
own-corpus cross-check
      ↓
closest-work search
      ↓
external targeted literature search
      ↓
novelty falsification
      ↓
experiment feasibility
      ↓
surviving candidate
```

## Status vocabulary

```
CANDIDATE
PARTIALLY_ADDRESSED
STILL_OPEN
LIKELY_CLOSED
CLOSED
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
ENGINEERING_ONLY
```

**"Not found in this repository" is not by itself grounds to promote an
observation to a research gap.** It must clear the own-corpus and
closest-work checks above first.

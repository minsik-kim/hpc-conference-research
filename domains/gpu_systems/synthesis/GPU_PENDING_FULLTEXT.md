## What this document is

Candidates that look valuable but **could not be analysed, because the full-paper gate was not met**:
the cluster briefs allow a *verdict* from a title, an abstract or an artifact, but forbid a deep analysis
unless the paper text was read. Every row below is a paper the corpus has judged worth reading and has not read.

**Inclusion rule.** Every ledger row whose verdict is `CORE_GPU`, or `UNRESOLVED` where the ledger states a
substantive reason for leaving it open, **and** whose access state is not `PUBLIC_FULLTEXT`; plus every
`PUBLIC_FULLTEXT` row a ledger explicitly flags as public and unread for capacity (16 rows, marked **PUBLIC — UNREAD**).

**Grounding rules this document is bound by**

- `Why candidate` is the **mechanism reason recorded in the ledger** — the row's own counterfactual answer and
  rationale. Nothing here is an invented description, and **no mechanism is inferred from an abstract**
  (`governance/ANTI_HALLUCINATION_RULES.md`: GPU-initiated vs host-initiated distinctions require the paper or the code).
  Where a ledger states the claim rests on the title plus a census row, the cell **says so in bold**.
- `Current access state` is carried over **as recorded**, not re-derived. Where two ledgers disagree, both are
  quoted and the row says so — `governance/KNOWLEDGE_BASE_RULES.md` rule 6 forbids silent reconciliation.
- `Recheck source` is a concrete thing to retry. `NO KNOWN SOURCE` is used literally, and means the census
  recorded `NOT_FOUND_AFTER_SEARCH` and no DOI, preprint, author PDF or artifact was located — **not** that the
  paper is unimportant.
- Access states are the ones observed **from this environment**. `CLOSED_ACCESS` here is frequently an
  access-*path* limitation, not a licence statement — see *Blocked by tooling, not by licence*.

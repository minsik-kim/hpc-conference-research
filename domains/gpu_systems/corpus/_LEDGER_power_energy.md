## 0. Reading notes that apply to the whole ledger

1. **The counterfactual test was applied strictly.** Three papers whose subject is
   GPU power but whose *mechanism* treats the GPU as a black box with a frequency
   knob are recorded `RELATED_GPU` and **not** deep-analysed, even where the full
   text was read: **throttLL'eM**, **EVeREST** and **TAPAS**. The bar for `CORE_GPU`
   in this cluster is that the contribution depends on a named GPU-specific
   property — the NVML/`nvidia-smi` sensor's own averaging behaviour and update
   latency, per-rail or per-package sensors on an APU, SM/CU clock domains and
   their thermal throttling, HBM vs core power domains, Tensor-Core-driven
   transients, or the GPU's own hardware power-capping controller and its latency.

2. **Stable-ID band.** This cluster used **181–187**. IDs 188–199 in the
   `GPU-<VENUE><YY>-<NN>` scheme remain unused by this pass.

3. **Access-path facts for this session.** `dl.acm.org` → 403, `ieeexplore.ieee.org`
   → 418, `dblp.org` and `escholarship.org/search/` → robots-disallowed,
   `par.nsf.gov` → not attempted (robots). `arxiv.org/html/<id>vN` and author /
   institution PDFs worked. `api.semanticscholar.org` worked once and then
   rate-limited (429). Several ACM/IEEE-only papers are `CLOSED_ACCESS` **as an
   access-path fact about this environment**, not a claim about their licence.

4. **Two arXiv preprints carry different titles from their published versions.**
   Recorded here so no future pass re-runs the search:
   - SC '24 *Accurate and Convenient Energy Measurements for GPUs* = arXiv
     **2312.02741** *"Part-time Power Measurements: nvidia-smi's Lack of Attention"*
     (linked by Semantic Scholar's DOI record, `DBLP:conf/sc/YangAA24`, identical
     authors and identical abstract numbers). **This corrects
     `census/SC_2024.md:44`, which recorded `NOT_FOUND_AFTER_SEARCH (no arXiv)`.**
   - ASPLOS '24 *Characterizing Power Management Opportunities for LLMs in the
     Cloud* = arXiv **2308.12908** *"POLCA: Power Oversubscription in LLM Cloud
     Providers"* (identical seven authors; the Microsoft Research publication page
     for the ASPLOS title names POLCA as the framework and hosts the archival PDF).

5. **`domains/hpc_systems_operations/` is an imported corpus** and already covered
   four of these papers, in all four cases shallowly. Those four files carry
   `GPU_DELTA_ANALYSIS` and open with an explicit delta notice. See §3.

6. **`domains/ai_hpc_systems/` is `EXTERNAL_IMPORT_PENDING` (not imported).** Every
   LLM-serving power paper below therefore carries
   `KNOWN_EXTERNAL_CORPUS_NOT_IMPORTED` and **no claim is made** about what that
   corpus does or does not hold.

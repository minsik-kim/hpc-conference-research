### 1.1 Population evidence notes

**The discrepancy, stated plainly and left open.** The official SC25 program announcement says **137 papers accepted**. The printed main volume contains **144 papers**, of which **133** are in the regular technical-paper DOI/page block and **11** are Gordon Bell class. 133 + 11 = 144, so the volume is internally consistent, but neither 133 nor 144 equals 137. Possible explanations — a withdrawal between notification and publication, Gordon Bell papers being counted inside or outside the 137, or the 137 including items that appear under a different DOI block — are **not** established by any source I read. I therefore report both figures and use **133** as the STEP-B screening denominator, because that is the enumerated set of regular technical papers actually in the volume. Do not treat 137, 133 or 144 as interchangeable.

**Why the 133/144 enumeration is exact.** Per the prior in-repo census `[proceedings]`: the date-filtered Crossref query returned `total-results = 433` and paging recovered 433 unique DOIs with zero duplicates (181 main + 252 workshop). Sorting the 144 main-volume papers by first page yields a perfectly contiguous run in which each paper's `last_page + 1` equals the next paper's `first_page`, with no gap or overlap across pages 1–2265. A missing paper would necessarily leave a page gap; there is none. I re-verified the container and a 67-record author sample against Crossref today; every sampled DOI matched `main_papers.txt`.

**Access state.** SC25 is an ACM volume and a substantial share of it appears to be open access in the ACM DL: search results surfaced `dl.acm.org/doi/full/…` and `dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/…` URLs for several papers (e.g. HELM, MXBLAS, AGILE, the CG communication paper). `dl.acm.org` returns 403 to WebFetch from this environment, so I could not confirm the per-paper licence; where a `doi/full` or `doi/pdf` URL was observed in a search result it is recorded as `ACM_OA?` (observed URL form, licence unconfirmed).

**Blocked sources (do not retry):** `dblp.org` (robots), `dl.acm.org` (403), `sc25.supercomputing.org/proceedings/tech_paper/` (404), `export.arxiv.org` + `arxiv.org/search/` (robots), `sc25.conference-program.com` (client-side-rendered Linklings — no session content to WebFetch, though `presentation/?id=papNNN&sess=sessNNN` URLs exist and are search-indexed).

**Authors.** Crossref rate-limited (HTTP 429) part-way through the author sweep; a first author is recorded only where actually read. Sessions are `UNKNOWN` throughout (Linklings not fetchable).

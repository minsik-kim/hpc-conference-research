### 1.1 Population evidence notes

- Source statement: the official program lists 7 main-conference technical sessions across days 2–4 containing, in order: Numerical Methods (4), Model Training (4), Storage Systems (3), Efficiency and Sustainability (4), Clouds and FaaS (3), Tuning and Performance (4), Best Paper and Best Student Paper Finalists (3). **4+4+3+4+3+4+3 = 25.**
- Source statement: every paper is listed with its complete author list and a session chair; all 25 titles/author lists were read from that page.
- Source statement: the program marks awards inline — `[BEST PAPER]` on *Out-of-Core Parallel Spatial Join…* and `[BEST STUDENT PAPER]` on *LegoIndex…*.
- **No official acceptance statistic ("X of Y") was found.** Neither `program.html`, nor `calls-cfp.html`, nor the site home page states a submission count or acceptance rate. The proceedings front matter is behind `dl.acm.org` (403). Recorded as `NOT_FOUND_AFTER_SEARCH`.
- Publisher corroboration attempted and **failed**: `dl.acm.org` 403; `dblp.org` robots-disallowed; `api.crossref.org` / `api.openalex.org` HTTP 429 on every attempt. Count therefore rests on one official source, extracted once in full.
- **Per-paper DOIs are UNKNOWN for every 2025 paper.** Only the proceedings-level DOI 10.1145/3731545 is known, and that from the official site's own link (so, unlike 2024, its year binding **is** verified).

## 3. Non-candidates with GPU keywords (screened out at STEP B)

| Title | Why not a GPU candidate |
|---|---|
| Virtualization So Light, it Floats! Accelerating Floating Point Virtualization | "Accelerating" keyword; the subject is **host/CPU floating-point virtualization** (same group's FP-virtualization line, continued at HPDC 2026), not a GPU |
| F3: An FPGA-accelerated FaaS Framework | Serverless/sharing keywords, but the accelerator is explicitly an **FPGA** |
| AutoSSD: CXL-Enhanced Autonomous SSDs for Low Tail Latency | Memory keyword; the device is a **CXL-attached SSD**, not a GPU |
| DPU-KV: On the Benefits of DPU Offloading for In-Memory Key-Value Stores at the Edge | Memory/RDMA-adjacent keywords, but the offload target is a **DPU/SmartNIC**, not a GPU |
| Grudon: A System for Deploying Graph Workloads on Disaggregated Architectures with Near-Data Processing | Memory keyword; the mechanism is **near-data processing on disaggregated memory**, not GPU |
| Out-of-Core Parallel Spatial Join Outperforming In-Memory Systems: A BFS-DFS Hybrid Approach | "In-Memory"/memory keyword; CPU out-of-core spatial-join algorithm |
| Advancing Scientific Data Compression via Cross-Field Prediction | Compression keyword; a **prediction-model/compression-ratio** contribution — no GPU mechanism identifiable from the title and the abstract was unreadable (flagged in §6) |
| IPComp: Interpolation Based Progressive Lossy Compression for Scientific Applications | Compression keyword; interpolation-based progressive compression — no GPU mechanism identifiable from the title; abstract unreadable (flagged in §6) |

| Top-Down SBP: Turning Graph Clustering Upside Down | No GPU keyword in title; graph-clustering (stochastic block partitioning) algorithm — listed here only because the authoring group publishes GPU variants; **no GPU evidence in any readable source** (flagged in §6) |
| HYPERF: End-to-End Autotuning Framework for High-Performance Computing | No GPU keyword; autotuning framework — target devices unstated and unreadable (flagged in §6) |

**Population accounting:** 8 candidates (§2) + 11 keyword-bearing non-candidates (§3) = 19. The remaining **6** papers carry no screening keyword at all and were screened out without a §3 row: *ModelX: A Novel Transfer Learning Approach Across Heterogeneous Datasets*; *CarbonEdge: Leveraging Mesoscale Spatial Carbon-Intensity Variations for Low Carbon Edge Computing*; *PathFence: Reducing Cross-Path Dependencies in Microservices*; *Privacy-preserving of HPC Traces for Data Sharing and Analysis*; *Weight-Sharing NAS with Architecture-Agnostic Intermediate Representation*; *LegoIndex: A Scalable and Modular Indexing Framework for Efficient Analysis of Extreme-Scale Particle Data*. 19 + 6 = **25**.

### 5.1 Candidates the seed list missed

6 of the 8 candidates in §2 are absent from the seed list, including the
**title-explicit** *FluidFaaS: … with Strong Isolation-based GPU Sharing*, which
is arguably the most central GPU-systems paper of HPDC 2025 and is a direct
follow-on to the HPDC 2024 seed paper *ESG* by overlapping authors (Xinning Hui,
Yuanchao Xu, Xipeng Shen). The seed list covered 2 of 8 candidates.

- Method note: the ISC agenda is a JavaScript Swapcard application. Individual `…/planning/<base64 id>` pages ARE server-rendered for URLs the search index has crawled, and were read one by one. Neighbouring ids in the same block were checked and found to be Focus Sessions, panels, the TOP500 session, the Exhibition Opening Reception, or items belonging to other (non-ISC) Swapcard events — confirming the Research Paper session block was fully bounded.
- **Full-text status for the whole population:** every ISC 2024 research paper is **open access at IEEE Xplore** (official ISC statement, quoted above). Per-paper IEEE URLs/DOIs could not be harvested because `ieeexplore.ieee.org` returns HTTP 418 through this proxy; the two IEEE document numbers recorded in §2 came from search-engine result titles, not from the pages themselves.
- No abstracts are exposed on the ISC 2024 Research Paper session pages, so STEP B screening for 2024 is **title-, author-, affiliation- and session-label-based**. Every inferential inclusion is tagged `[TITLE-ONLY]`.

## 6. Unresolved / blocked items

- **IEEE Xplore unreachable.** `ieeexplore.ieee.org` returns **HTTP 418** for both `/xpl/conhome/10528919/proceeding` and `/document/<id>/` through this proxy. Consequence: **no per-paper DOIs and no per-paper official publisher URLs for any ISC 2024 research paper.** Only two IEEE document numbers (10528931, 10528923) were recoverable, and only from search-engine result titles.
- **dblp** (`dblp.org`, `dblp.dagstuhl.de`) robots-disallowed; **dl.acm.org** 403; **Crossref REST API** returned `total-results: 0` for `container-title:"ISC High Performance 2024 Research Paper Proceedings"` (IEEE proceedings metadata not retrievable that way); **scholar.archive.org** rate-limited; **fatcat.wiki** robots.txt fetch timeout; **scilit.com** 403; **ouci.dntb.gov.ua** search form not addressable by URL. So no independent bibliographic index could corroborate the TOC.
- **No abstracts** are exposed on ISC 2024 Research Paper session pages (unlike some 2025/2026 pages), so all 2024 STEP B judgments are `[TITLE-ONLY]`.
- **Artifact/code URLs**: searched only for the four GPU-*mechanism* candidates (#1, #2, #3, #8) per the budget prioritisation rule; all four `NOT_FOUND_AFTER_SEARCH`. Candidates #4, #5, #6, #7 are `NOT_SEARCHED (deprioritised)`.
- Excluded-class counts (tutorials, BoFs, posters, PhD Forum, HPC in Asia, Industrial Day) are UNKNOWN: the official ISC 2024 pages reached do not publish per-class counts, and the agenda cannot be enumerated in bulk.

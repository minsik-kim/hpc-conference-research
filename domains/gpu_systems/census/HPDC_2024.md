### 1.1 Population evidence notes

- Source statement: the official program lists 8 main-conference technical sessions on days 3–5 (5–7 June) containing, in order: Compression and AI (4), Resilience (3), Serverless Computing (3), Software Systems & Workflows (3), Performance modeling & LLMs (3), Accelerators & Scientific applications (3), Resource Management (3), Communication & Storage (4). **4+3+3+3+3+3+3+4 = 26.**
- Source statement: the program page lists each paper with its full author list; all 26 titles and author lists were read directly from that page.
- **No official acceptance statistic ("X of Y") was found.** `calls-cfp.html`, the program page and the site home page contain none. The proceedings front-matter ("Message from the Chairs"), which normally carries it, is on `dl.acm.org`, which returns 403. Recorded as `NOT_FOUND_AFTER_SEARCH`.
- Publisher corroboration attempted and **failed**: `dl.acm.org` returns 403; `www.conference-publishing.com/toc/HPDC24/noabs` returns "Problem with empty event record" (HPDC does not use conference-publishing.com); `dblp.org` is robots-disallowed; `api.crossref.org` and `api.openalex.org` returned HTTP 429 to every request through the available proxy. So the count rests on **one** official source, read twice with two different extraction prompts that agreed exactly.
- The ACM proceedings DOI for HPDC 2024 is most likely **10.1145/3625549** (the URL `https://dl.acm.org/doi/proceedings/10.1145/3625549` was returned by a search engine under the title "HPDC: High Performance Distributed Computing" when querying HPDC 2024 accepted papers). Unlike 2025 and 2026, the official 2024 site carries **no** dl.acm.org link, so the year binding of this DOI is **UNVERIFIED** — `source class: search-engine`.
- **Per-paper DOIs are UNKNOWN for every 2024 paper** (ACM DL 403; Crossref 429). They are recorded as `UNKNOWN` rather than reconstructed.
- Inference (not a source statement): 26 is slightly above HPDC's usual ~20–25 full papers; nothing in the program suggests any of the 26 is a short or invited paper.

### 1.1 Population evidence notes

- Source statement: the official accepted-papers page enumerates exactly 45 titles, numbered 1–45, each with a full author list.
- Source statement: the official program page places papers in these sessions — Session 2 "Best Paper Nominees" (5); 3A "Memory and Storage Systems" (4); 3B "Emerging Supercomputing Applications" (4); 5A "Reliability, Dependability and Availability" (4); 5B "Heterogeneous Software: GPUs and Domain Specific Accelerators" (4); 6A "Cloud and ML Systems Efficiency" (4); 6B "Accelerator Designs" (4); 8A "Supercomputing Software and Security" (4); 8B "Interconnects and Networks" (4); 9A "Machine Learning Systems" (4); 9B "Software Design for Accelerators" (4). Sum = 45.
- Inference: two independent official enumerations agree at 45 and their title sets coincide, therefore the population is EXACT.
- **No official acceptance statistic of the form "X of Y submissions" was located.** The official accepted-papers page, the program page and https://www.ics-conference.org/archive.html carry none. WebSearch budget was exhausted before an alternative statistics source could be queried (see §6).
- Publisher cross-check not possible: `dl.acm.org` returns 403 and `dblp.org` is robots-disallowed. A WebSearch result title identified the ACM proceedings as "Proceedings of the 38th ACM International Conference on Supercomputing", DOI prefix `10.1145/3650200` (`search-engine` source statement); the table of contents itself could not be opened, so **per-paper DOIs are UNKNOWN**.
- No abstracts are published on either official ICS 2024 page, and no abstract source was reachable (see §6). STEP B screening for this year is therefore **title-, author- and session-label-based**; every inferential inclusion is tagged.

### 1.1 Population evidence notes

- Two independent enumeration passes over the official program agreed: a per-session breakdown summing to 135, and a single continuously numbered list of all paper titles ending at **135**. 135 − 4 Industry Track = **131**.
- Publisher corroboration of the *total* was not obtainable (`dl.acm.org` 403, `dblp.org` robots-disallowed), but per-paper DOI lookups confirm the proceedings DOI prefix for ISCA 2025 is `10.1145/3695053.*` (ACM), and every candidate checked resolved to a real ACM DL record.
- **Open-access observation (inferred, not stated by a source):** for many ISCA 2025 papers the search index exposes `dl.acm.org/doi/full/...` and `dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/...` URLs and Unpaywall records, which is consistent with a substantial portion of the ISCA 2025 proceedings being open access under ACM OPEN. This is an inference from URL shapes, not a statement any source made.
- Not established: whether any accepted paper is absent from the program page.

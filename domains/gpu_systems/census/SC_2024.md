### 1.1 Population evidence notes

The prior in-repo census (`SC_2024_2025_QUANTUM_HPC_CENSUS.md` §2.4) established the SC24 denominator by exhaustive Crossref container enumeration: a contiguous, gap-free DOI run `.00001`–`.00114`, 114 records, zero gaps, zero duplicates, with `.00115` returning 404 — so `.00114` is the last item. `[proceedings]` I did not re-run the full enumeration; I re-verified the container identity and a 64-record sample of titles/authors against Crossref today, and every sampled suffix/title pair matched `sc24_main.tsv` exactly. No discrepancy was found.

Acceptance rate: `NOT_FOUND_AFTER_SEARCH`. The prior census records that no official SC24 submission/acceptance figure was obtainable, and I found none. Do not infer 99/N.

Blocked sources (do not retry): `dblp.org` (robots), `dl.acm.org` (403 to WebFetch), `sc24.supercomputing.org/proceedings/` (robots-disallowed — the per-paper pages such as `…/proceedings/tech_paper/tech_paper_pages/pap649.html` exist and are indexed by search engines, but the directory is not fetchable), `export.arxiv.org` and `arxiv.org/search/` (robots). `arxiv.org/abs/` and `arxiv.org/html/` are fetchable. `sc24.conference-program.com` is a client-side-rendered Linklings browser and returns no session content to WebFetch, though individual `presentation/?id=papNNN&sess=sessNNN` URLs exist and are search-indexed.

**Authors.** Crossref rate-limited (HTTP 429) before the full author sweep completed, so a first author is recorded only where it was actually read from a source; the rest are `UNKNOWN`. Sessions were not obtainable (Linklings not fetchable) and are `UNKNOWN` throughout.

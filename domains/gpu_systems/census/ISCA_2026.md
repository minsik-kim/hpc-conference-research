### 1.1 Population evidence notes

- The official program was enumerated twice, and the passes **disagreed**: a per-session counting pass reported a 160 total (regular) + 11 (industry), while a single continuously numbered enumeration of every paper title ended at **172**. Re-checking the per-session pass against the titles it had itself listed showed it under-counted four sessions (7A, 7B, 7D, 10C each had 6, 6, 6, 5 titles listed but were reported as 5, 5, 5, 4). The continuously numbered enumeration (172) is therefore treated as authoritative.
- 172 program paper entries − 11 Industry Track = **161** main/regular research papers.
- The ISCA 2026 proceedings DOI prefix is **IEEE**, `10.1109/ISCA66397.2026.*` (confirmed by four DOIs listed on the Yonsei ESCAL lab publication page) — unlike ISCA 2025, which used ACM `10.1145/3695053.*`.
- The IEEE CS CSDL proceedings TOC for ISCA 2026 could not be reached, so per-paper DOIs are known only for the handful recovered individually. `dl.acm.org` (403) and `dblp.org` (robots-disallowed) were also unavailable.
- Not established: whether any accepted paper is absent from the program page.

### 1.1 Population evidence notes

SC26 has not yet taken place (November 2026) and its proceedings do not exist. Per `sc26.supercomputing.org/program/papers/`, paper notifications went out on **1 July 2026** and the "online SC Schedule" was to be available by September 2026. The schedule **does** exist at `sc26.conference-program.com`, but it is a client-side-rendered Linklings browser: a server-side fetch returns only the filter chrome and the string *"No presentations match the current selections."* The site's `sitemap.xml` indexes only `page-sitemap.xml` and exposes no `/presentation/` or `/session/` URLs, and `https://sc26.conference-program.com/presentation/` returns the site's not-found page. `sc26.supercomputing.org/proceedings/?type=papers` states only "Available November 2026".

Attempts that failed (do not retry): `ssl.linklings.net/conferences/sc/…` (robots-disallowed), `sc26.conference-program.com/?filter=Papers` and `?searchQuery=GPU` (no server-rendered content), `dblp.org` (robots), `dl.acm.org` (403), `arxiv.org/search/` and `export.arxiv.org` (robots), site-restricted search for `sc26.conference-program.com` presentation pages (nothing indexed).

**Consequence for STEP B.** The screening below is therefore **not** over a reconstructed population. It is over two sources: (a) the 9 officially confirmed finalist papers, and (b) arXiv preprints located by searching for the seed titles. For every item in group (b) the SC26 membership is tagged `SC26_MEMBERSHIP_UNVERIFIED` — an arXiv preprint whose title matches a seed is **not** evidence that the paper is an SC26 main-track paper. Nothing here should be read as a count of SC26 GPU papers.

### 1.1 Population evidence notes

- Source statement: the official program lists **12 numbered main-conference technical sessions** across days 2–4 containing, in order: S1 LLM Inference: Scheduling & Serving (3), S2 Scaling Generative Inference (3), S3 MoE Systems & Distributed LLM Training (3), S4 Programming Models, Frameworks & Compilers (4), S5 Scientific Applications at Scale (4), S6 Scientific Data Compression (4), S7 Graph Processing & Community Detection (3), S8 Storage & I/O for Data-Intensive Workloads (4), S9 Cloud, Serverless & Microservices (3), S10 Data Systems for AI & Analytics (3), S11 Communication, Workflows & Sustainability (4), S12 Emerging Generative & Agentic AI Workloads (2). **3+3+3+4+4+4+3+4+3+3+4+2 = 40.**
- Source statement: every paper is listed with its complete author list and a session chair with e-mail; all 40 titles/author lists were read from that page.
- Verification note: a first extraction of the program reported a *total* of "41" while its own per-session list summed to 40. The whole of **day 4 was then re-fetched verbatim** (table rows, not a summary) and confirms S9=3, S10=3, S11=4, S12=2, followed directly by "Coffee Break" and "Awards Announcements and Closing Remarks" — i.e. **Session 12 really contains only 2 papers** and nothing follows it. The "41" was an arithmetic error in the extraction, not a missing paper. **The count is 40.**
- Source statement: awards are marked inline — **Best Paper Winner**: *Pome: Parallelizing I/Os and Computations for LSM-tree Storage*; **Best Paper Award Nominees**: *CARBS: Compiler Autotuning via Randomized Biased Search* and *PowerQuant: Architecture-Agnostic GPU Power Estimation via Quantile Regression*.
- **No official acceptance statistic ("X of Y") was found.** `program.html`, `calls-cfp.html` and the home page state none. The proceedings front matter is behind `dl.acm.org` (403). Recorded as `NOT_FOUND_AFTER_SEARCH`.
- Inference (not a source statement): 40 main-track papers is a **large jump** from HPDC 2024 (26) and HPDC 2025 (25) and well above the venue's historical ~20–25. The program structure (12 numbered sessions vs 8 in 2024 and 7 in 2025) is consistent with a genuinely larger program rather than with a mis-read. Nothing in the program marks any of the 40 as short, invited or industry papers.
- **Per-paper DOIs are UNKNOWN for every 2026 paper.** Only the proceedings-level DOI 10.1145/3806645 is known (verified from the official site's own link).

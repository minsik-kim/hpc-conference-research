### 1.1 Population evidence notes

**What was retrieved from the official site.** The official program (2026.hpca-conf.org, a
researchr instance; conference held 2–4 Feb 2026) renders every day on one very large page. The
fetcher truncated it consistently. Fully retrieved:

- Monday 2 Feb: 12 sessions × 4 papers = **48 papers**, all titles captured (Cache Coherence and
  Chiplet Interconnects; Best Paper Candidates; Homomorphic Encryption Acceleration; DRAM Security
  and Reliability; Near-Data Processing and Storage; Quantum Computing Architecture; Memory System
  Reliability; LLM Inference Serving Systems; Quantum Compilation and Simulation;
  Processing-in-Memory Architectures; Efficient LLM Inference Techniques; 3D Graphics and Rendering
  Acceleration).
- Tuesday 3 Feb (partial): CPU Microarchitecture Optimization (4), Wafer-Scale Systems for Large
  Models (4), Best of CAL (3, excluded). Truncation occurred at the start of "Caching and
  Prefetching".
- Wednesday 4 Feb: **not retrieved.**
- Industry Track (3) and Best of CAL (3) retrieved completely from their own track pages.

Attempts that failed and were not retried: `?date=`-filtered program URLs (robots-disallowed);
conf.researchr.org mirror (same truncation); api.openalex.org and api.semanticscholar.org (HTTP
429); arxiv.org/search and export.arxiv.org (robots-disallowed); dblp.org (robots-disallowed);
dl.acm.org (403); IEEE CSDL (page scaffolding only).

**Bibliographic corroboration.** https://researchr.org/publication/hpca-2026 (DBLP-derived) was
fetched twice. One fetch enumerated **116 distinct titles with first authors**; the other reported
a total of **126 entries** on the same page. These disagree, and a title known to be in the
proceedings from a third-party reading list ("RoMe: Row Granularity Access Memory System for Large
Language Models") is absent from the 116 enumerated, so the 116-item enumeration is demonstrably
incomplete. openaccept.org separately reports **119 accepted** main-research-track papers out of
602 submissions.

**Inference, stated as inference:** the regular-research population is most likely in the
113–123 range, with 119 the single most plausible value because it is the only figure that is an
*accepted-paper count* rather than a rendering-dependent TOC count. No single figure is asserted
as the population. `census_status` is therefore `PARTIAL_CENSUS`.

Cycle structure: HPCA 2026 ran two submission cycles. Neither the program nor the proceedings TOC
labels papers by cycle, so no per-cycle split is claimed.

**What the enumeration does support.** 116 titles with first authors are known from the
proceedings TOC, 48 titles are confirmed session-placed from the official Monday program, and all
seed items were located. GPU screening below was applied over the union of the 116 enumerated
proceedings titles and the captured official-program titles — i.e. over an estimated ~95 % of the
population, not 100 %.

### 1.1 Population evidence notes

- The official program page was enumerated twice. A first pass produced a per-session breakdown; a second pass produced a single continuously numbered list of every paper title ending at **87**. The continuously numbered enumeration is treated as authoritative because per-session summation passes produced arithmetic errors (one pass asserted "approximately 79", another asserted "98"; both are inconsistent with the per-session counts they themselves listed, which sum to 87).
- 87 program paper entries − 4 Industry Session papers = **83** main/regular research papers.
- Publisher-side corroboration of the *total* was not obtainable: `dl.acm.org` returns 403 to fetching, `dblp.org` is disallowed by robots.txt, and no ISCA 2024 proceedings TOC was reachable. The 83 figure therefore rests on the official program enumeration alone, with per-paper DOI spot checks confirming that individual program entries do exist in the published proceedings.
- Not established (source did not say): whether the published proceedings contain any paper that was accepted but not scheduled in the program page's sessions. No claim is made either way.

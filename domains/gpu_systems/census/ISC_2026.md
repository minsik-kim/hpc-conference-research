### 1.1 Population evidence notes

Enumerated from the official ISC 2026 program (2 of 13):

1. **Hans Meuer Research Paper Award Session** — Wed 24 Jun 2026, 11:15–11:45, Hall 4 (Ground Floor), type `Research Paper`
   - "Hans Meuer Award Ceremony and Presentation: **PICO: Performance Insights for Collective Operations**" — Tommaso Bonato, Lorenzo Piarulli, Torsten Hoefler, Marco Canini, Daniele De Sensi; speaker Saverio Pasqualoni (KAUST / Sapienza University of Rome). Official abstract on the page: an open-source collective-benchmarking framework that "decouples portable experiment setup from platform execution", supports **MPI and NCCL backends**, and finds default algorithms "up to 5x slower than the best available choice", with LLM-training simulations showing "reductions in training times of up to 44%".
2. **Best Student Research Paper Award Session** — Tue 23 Jun 2026, 17:15–17:45, type `Research Paper`
   - "**Energy Efficiency in Analog Photonic Processors: Conversions and Losses at Scale**" — Niklas Bahr (Volkswagen Group Innovation / Kirchhoff-Institute for Physics, Heidelberg), Daniel Steinmeyer, Wolfram Pernice. Page states the full paper is on IEEE Xplore (Open Access).

Cross-check on plausibility of the count: the 13-paper figure is consistent with the seed list — the seed list plus the two award papers name at most ~12 distinct ISC-2026-linked titles, which fits inside 13.

- Method note (why enumeration stopped): individual Swapcard planning ids were probed across the ISC 2026 id space (4390xxx, 4395xxx, 44099xx, 4410xxx, 44434xx). Ids 4443481/4443482 (the two award sessions) render because a search index has crawled them; adjacent ids either render as items from *other* Swapcard events or return only the login shell. Blind id scanning therefore cannot enumerate uncrawled ISC 2026 sessions, and repeated domain-restricted searches for "Research Paper Session" + ISC 2026 returned only 2024/2025 sessions.

## 6. Unresolved / blocked items

- **11 of the 13 ISC 2026 research paper titles are unenumerated.** Blockers, each confirmed this session:
  - `ieeexplore.ieee.org` → **HTTP 418** on `/xpl/conhome/...` and `/document/<id>/`. IEEE is the publisher of record for ISC research papers (open access), so this removes the only authoritative TOC.
  - Official ISC 2026 agenda = JavaScript Swapcard app. The sessions-list endpoints (`.../plannings/RXZlbnRWaWV3XzEyMDQ5NDY=` and its widget form) render only filter controls, never the session list. Individual `.../planning/<id>` pages render only when previously crawled; uncrawled ids return the login shell. Domain-restricted searches for ISC 2026 Research Paper sessions returned only 2024/2025 sessions.
  - `isc-hpc.com/program/` and `/schedule/` carry no paper titles and only link out to the Swapcard app. The ISC 2026 post-event news item gives attendance (4,035 from 64 countries) and exhibitors (188 from 26 countries) but **no paper statistics**.
  - `isc-hpc.com/submissions/research-paper/` statistics table has **no ISC 2026 row** (0/0 placeholder).
  - dblp (`dblp.org`, `dblp.dagstuhl.de`) robots-disallowed; `dl.acm.org` 403; Crossref REST API `total-results: 0` for the ISC container title; `scholar.archive.org` rate-limited; `fatcat.wiki` robots.txt timeout; `scilit.com` 403; `ouci.dntb.gov.ua` search not URL-addressable; `link.springer.com` has only ISC workshop volumes (2024 = LNCS 15058, 2025 = LNCS 16091) and no ISC 2026 volume at last check.
- **Discrepancy to flag:** 13 accepted of 122 submitted (10.7%) is a sharp break from 2023–2025 (21/78, 24/80, 28/97 ≈ 27–29%). The 13/122 figures were quoted verbatim from the official press release and are recorded as-is; if ISC 2026 in fact ran more than one submission round, the 13 may cover only one round. **This could not be resolved from any source reached.**
- **Author lists** for candidates #3–#6 are UNKNOWN: arXiv `/abs/<id>` markdown conversion strips the Title/Authors block in this environment, so rows #3–#6 were matched on title/abstract text, and only #2 (read via `/html/`) yielded an author list.
- **Session assignment, DOI and official URL are NOT_RETRIEVED for candidates #2–#6** — they depend on the official program pages or IEEE Xplore, both blocked.
- **Artifact/code URLs:** searched only for the GPU-*mechanism* candidates (#1 collectives, #2 power attribution, #3 utilization). #2 yielded https://github.com/hpc-ai-adv-dev/fastotf2; #1 and #3 are `NOT_FOUND_AFTER_SEARCH`. #4, #5, #6 are `NOT_SEARCHED (deprioritised)`.

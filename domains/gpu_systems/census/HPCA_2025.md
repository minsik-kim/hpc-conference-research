### 1.1 Population evidence notes

Source statement: the official main-program page enumerates **121 paper entries across 35
sessions** over three days (Mon 3 – Wed 5 March 2025). Two independent enumerations of the same
page returned the same per-session counts:

- Day 1: 1A 3, 1B 3, 1C 3, 2A 3, 2B 3, 2C 3, 3A 4, 3B 3 (Best of CAL), 3C 4, 3D 4, 4A 3, 4B 3, 4C 3, 5A 3, 5B 3, 5C 3 = 51
- Day 2: 6A 3, 6B 3, 6C 3, 7A 3, 7B 3, 7C 3, 8A 3, 8B 3, 8C 3, 9A 5, 9B 5 (Industry), 9C 5 = 42
- Day 3: 10A 4, 10B 4, 10C 4, 10D 4, 11A 4, 11B 4, 11C 4 = 28

Inference: 121 − 3 (Best of CAL) − 5 (Industry Session) = **113** regular research papers.

Corroboration: the DBLP-derived researchr proceedings TOC contains the same items in the same
composition (its fetched rendering was truncated after 116 of the entries — the five missing tail
entries are exactly NearFetch, PAISE, FACIL, Lincoln and "Make LLM Inference Affordable to
Everyone", which restores the 121 total). The Best of CAL items in that TOC are "The Importance of
Generalizability in Machine Learning for Systems", "R.I.P. Geomean Speedup ..." and "eDKM ...";
the Industry items are MLPerf Power, Enterprise Class Modular Cache Hierarchy, Predicting
DRAM-Caused Risky VMs, Enhancing Large-Scale AI Training Efficiency (C4) and Revisiting
Reliability in Large-Scale ML Research Clusters.

No published acceptance statistic for HPCA 2025 was read in this session, so 113 rests on the
official program plus the proceedings TOC only.

Cycle structure: HPCA 2025 ran two submission cycles. The official program does **not** label
papers by cycle, so a single main-program total is reported and no per-cycle split is claimed.

Title normalisation notes (proceedings TOC vs program page; the proceedings form is treated as
official where they differ): "MASCOT" → "Mascot"; "SoMA" → "SoMa"; "GOPIM" → "GoPIM";
"HATT: Hamiltonian Aware Ternary Tree" → "HATT: Hamiltonian Adaptive Ternary Tree";
"MANT" → "M-ANT"; "Ariadne" (mobile compressed swap) keeps that spelling and is unrelated to the
HPCA 2026 GPU paper "ARIADNE".
